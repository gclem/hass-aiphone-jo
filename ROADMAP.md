# Roadmap

Upcoming features, in no particular order. Suggestions are welcome in the
issues.

- **Image on ring**: capture an image of the visitor when a station rings.
- **Call answering and talking to the visitor**: validate them on more
  installations and remove their experimental status.
- **Output status**: expose the state of the monitor outputs when the monitor
  reports it.
- **Doorbell detection**: confirm ring detection on more installations and
  remove its experimental status.
