# Roadmap

Upcoming features, in no particular order. Suggestions are welcome in the
issues.

- **Camera**: on-demand image and live stream of the entrance stations.
- **Call answering**: answer a ring from Home Assistant, with two-way audio.
- **Lock status**: expose the state of the lock outputs when the monitor
  reports it.
- **Doorbell detection**: confirm ring detection on more installations and
  remove its experimental status.
